Prof. Yanan Huang leads the **Yanan Lab for Nanomaterials & Self-Assembly** at Westfield University. Her group develops bottom-up strategies to engineer nanoparticles with programmable optical and chemical properties, applying these materials to challenges in diagnostics, drug delivery, and sustainable energy.

She teaches undergraduate Physical Chemistry and a graduate seminar in Nanoscale Materials. She is actively involved in mentoring students from underrepresented groups through the Westfield STEM Bridge Program.
