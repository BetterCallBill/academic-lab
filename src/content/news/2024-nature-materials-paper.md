We are excited to share that our paper **"DNA-Directed Three-Dimensional Assembly of Gold Nanorods into Chiral Superstructures with Programmable Optical Activity"** has been published in *Nature Materials*.

Led by postdoctoral researcher Dr. Sara Kim, with contributions from PhD student Luca Ferrari, this work demonstrates that DNA origami scaffolds can precisely position gold nanorods in three-dimensional helical arrangements. The resulting superstructures exhibit strong, tunable circular dichroism in the visible range — a property absent in isolated nanorods.

The work has potential applications in chiral sensing, enantioselective catalysis, and optical information storage.
