The Chen Lab had a strong presence at the **2023 Gordon Research Conference on Nanoscale Science and Engineering** held at Proctor Academy, New Hampshire (June 18–23).

Prof. Chen delivered an **invited talk** titled *"Programmable Optical Activity in DNA-Assembled Plasmonic Superstructures"* to an audience of 200+ researchers from 25 countries.

PhD student Luca Ferrari presented a **poster** on his work developing gap-enhanced gold nanostar dimers for SERS biosensing, which received the **Best Poster Award** from the conference organizing committee.

We thank the organizers for an outstanding scientific program and look forward to GRC 2025!
